About Me

My Journey

My story with technology didn't start in a classroom — it started with curiosity. I remember the first time I made a program do exactly what I imagined and thought: “if I can describe a problem precisely enough, a machine will solve it.” That idea never left me.

To pursue it seriously, I enrolled in two degrees simultaneously — a B.E. in Information Science & Engineering at Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering (CGPA 9.28) and a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Data Analytics at IIT Patna (CPI 9.0). One sharpened my engineering instincts: systems design, algorithms, and how to build things that scale. The other deepened my analytical thinking: statistics, ML, and how to extract meaning from data. Together they gave me a rare lens — I think in both code and math.

That duality shows up in everything I build. I engineered a metro routing system where a Java core (Dijkstra, A*, Yen’s K-shortest paths) talks to a live Flask + Random Forest service that adjusts edge weights in real time — cutting path search by ~64% and hitting sub-2ms computation. I built a bilingual SQL AI that reached 85% execution accuracy by combining RAG, reranking, and a sqlglot read-only guard. I shipped a mental health platform with a multilingual voice AI companion, JWT-secured sessions, and MongoDB-backed mood history. These aren’t prototypes — they have tests, CI, and real users.

Along the way I picked up a Runner-Up at MathWorks CodeSprint’24 (135 teams, 24 hours), earned a Super Contributor badge at Hacktoberfest 2025, and completed internships in Data Science and Generative AI. Every project, competition, and late-night debugging session has pushed me closer to the engineer I want to be: someone who ships production-grade systems, thinks deeply about trade-offs, and builds things that genuinely matter.
